HOW THIS IS COUNTED

  • Every graded CMMG pick in the range, wins and losses, newest first.
  • Pregame picks only, as published. Odds and stake as posted; your price may differ.

  • Voids and postponements are listed, not counted in the record.
  • Past results don't promise future results.
