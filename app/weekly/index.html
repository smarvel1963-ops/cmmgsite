HOW THIS IS COUNTED

  • Every graded Pro Track pick from Monday to Sunday (Central Time), wins and losses. The Free Pick is a separate record, shown on its own.
  • Units are each pick's result as graded, at its real payout. Breakeven is the win % the posted odds need.
  • "Your week" uses the picks you tapped "I Took This Pick" on, at the odds you entered.
  • Voids and postponements are listed, not counted in the record. Past results don't promise future results.
