Privacy Policy

CMMG Sports Edge · cmmgsports.com and the CMMG Sports Edge app · Last updated October 18, 2026

What we collect

  • Your Discord identity, when you sign in with Discord: your Discord user ID, display name and profile picture, and which CMMG Discord roles you have. We use them to show your name and to decide which picks your membership includes. We never see your Discord password.
  • Picks you track: when you tap "I Took This Pick", we save that pick with the odds, units, sportsbook and note you enter, so My Tracker can grade it.

That's all. No ads, no advertising trackers, no analytics, no contacts, no location, and no payment details. Payments for paid plans happen on Winible, outside this site and app, under Winible's own policy.

Where it's kept

Tracked picks are stored on CMMG's server (Google Apps Script) linked to your Discord user ID. Your sign-in is a signed token kept on your own device for up to 24 hours. Settings such as your preferred view are kept only in your browser. Everything travels over encrypted HTTPS.

Who sees it

Only you can see your tracker by name. Two exceptions, both described here:

  • Anonymous totals. The CMMG team sees combined numbers from everyone's trackers with no names attached - for example how many members took a pick, and members' combined results.
  • If you choose to share. When you turn on "Share my tracker with CMMG" in My Tracker, the CMMG team can see your Discord display name and the picks you mark (with your odds, units and results). Turn it off any time: your name is removed and your picks are no longer shown to us by name. They still count, without your name, in the anonymous totals.

We don't sell or share your data. The services that run the site (GitHub Pages for the website, Google for our server, Discord for sign-in) process it only to provide the service.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything we store about you at any time: open Settings & Membership → Delete my account & data (sign in first). It removes your tracked picks immediately. Your Discord account and roles are not affected. See how to delete your account, or email smarvel1963@gmail.com.

Age

CMMG Sports Edge is for adults 21+ (18+ where allowed). It is information and entertainment only. There is no wagering in this site or app. Gambling problem? Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
