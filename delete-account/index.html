Delete your CMMG Sports Edge account & data

This works for the CMMG Sports Edge app and for cmmgsports.com. It's the same account.

  1. Open Settings & Membership and sign in with the Discord account you use for CMMG.
  2. Scroll to Delete my account & data and tap it twice to confirm.
Go to Delete my account & data

What is deleted: every pick you saved to My Tracker, with your odds, units, sportsbook and notes. It's removed right away and can't be recovered. You're also signed out on that device.

What is not affected: your Discord account and Discord roles, and any paid plan on Winible. Cancel a plan on Winible.
