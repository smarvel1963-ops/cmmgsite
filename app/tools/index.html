📘 LEARN THE BASICS

  • Units — a unit is your standard bet size (say 1% of your bankroll). "2u" means twice your normal bet. Results in units let people with different bankrolls compare.
  • Break-even — at −110 you must win 52.4% just to break even. The bigger the favorite, the higher that bar. Every price has its own break-even.
  • CMMG tiers — ICE is the model's strongest tier, then EDGE, then FIRE. A tier says how strongly the model backs a pick, not that it will win.
  • Line shopping — the same bet is priced differently at different books. A few cents of price on every bet adds up over a season.
  • Parlays — every leg must win, and the book's cut stacks on each leg. That's why CMMG plays are almost all straight bets.
  • Bankroll — bet only money you can afford to lose, keep unit size steady, and never chase losses.
